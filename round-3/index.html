How Harper works

  1. Step 1

    You pay one price

    One flat price per employee, drawn from your bank account on the 1st. That is the whole bill.

  2. Step 2

    Your team gets credits

    Each employee gets health credits in their own account. They can see what they have and where to use it.

  3. Step 3

    They see a local doctor

    They pick a clinic in the Harper network. At the visit they get a one-time PIN and give it to the front desk.

  4. Step 4

    Harper pays the doctor

    The clinic enters the PIN and Harper pays them directly. Nobody files a claim.

What you can count on

  • One bill a month. The same price per person, drawn on the first.
  • No claim forms. Not for you, your team or the clinic.
  • No deductible to meet. A credit pays for the visit.
  • We store very little. Only a date of birth. Never what happened at a visit.

For doctors and clinics: new patients from local businesses

Harper sends you patients from small businesses in your part of DFW. At each visit you enter the patient's PIN. Harper pays you on the 15th of the next month. There is no insurance to bill.

Common questions

Not answered here? Email contact@harperhealthnetwork.com.

Is Harper insurance?

No. You buy health credits for your team. They spend them with local providers who take Harper. There is no plan, no network fine print and no claims.

How much does it cost?

One flat price per employee, each month. The price depends on the size of your team, so we send you a quote.

What can my team use credits for?

Visits with self-pay doctors and clinics in the Harper network. Each visit uses a credit.

Where does Harper work?

Dallas Fort Worth first, in five zip codes. Austin and Houston come next.

What do you know about my employees' health?

Almost nothing. We keep a date of birth. We never keep what happened at a visit.

How do providers get paid?

Harper pays them directly on the 15th of the next month, for every PIN they entered.
