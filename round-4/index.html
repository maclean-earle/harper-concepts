For providers

How Harper works

Four steps. Nobody files a claim at any of them.

  1. 1

    You pay one price

    One flat price per employee, billed once a month on the 1st. That is the whole bill.

  2. 2

    Your team gets credits

    Each employee gets health credits. They can see what they have and where to use it.

  3. 3

    They see a local doctor

    They pick a clinic in the Harper network. They get a code for the visit and give it to the front desk.

  4. 4

    Harper pays the clinic

    The clinic enters the code and Harper pays them directly, on the 15th of the next month.

What you can count on

  • One bill a month. The same price per person, billed on the 1st.
  • No claim forms. Not for you, your team or the clinic.
  • No deductible to meet. A credit pays for the visit.
  • We keep very little. Only a date of birth. Never what happened at a visit.

For doctors and clinics: new patients from local businesses

Harper sends you patients from small businesses in your part of DFW. At each visit the patient gives you a code. You enter it, and Harper pays you on the 15th of the next month.

There is no insurance to bill and no claim to chase.

Common questions

Not answered here? Email contact@harperhealthnetwork.com and a person at Harper writes back.

Is Harper insurance?

No. You buy health credits for your team. They spend them with local providers who take Harper. There is no plan, no network fine print and no claims.

How much does it cost?

One flat price per employee, each month. The price depends on the size of your team, so we send you a quote.

What can my team use credits for?

Visits with doctors and clinics in the Harper network, such as sick visits, check-ups and care for ongoing conditions. Each visit uses a credit.

Where does Harper work?

Dallas Fort Worth first, in five zip codes. Austin and Houston come next.

What do you know about my employees' health?

Almost nothing. We keep a date of birth. We never keep what happened at a visit.

How do providers get paid?

Harper pays them directly on the 15th of the next month, for every visit code they entered.
